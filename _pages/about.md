I am a physical oceanographer interested in geophysical fluid dynamics and ocean turbulence. I earned my Ph.D. in Earth, Environmental and Planetary Sciences from [Brown University](https://deeps.brown.edu/) in May 2026, advised by [Prof. Baylor Fox-Kemper](https://fox-kemper.com/). My dissertation, "Ocean Submesoscale Transport and Entrainment: Theory, Modeling, and Parameterization", focused on submesoscale dynamics, from the ice-covered polar oceans to the mid-latitude open-ocean conditions, with particular emphasis on the development of physics-based sub-grid-scale parameterizations of submesoscale transport and their implications for biogeochemical tracers. Before that, I earned a bachelor's degree in Physics from the University of Trento, Italy, and a master's degree in Physics of the Earth System from the University of Bologna, Italy, where I was advised by [Prof. Nadia Pinardi](https://www.cmcc.it/people/pinardi-nadia).

In my research, I use ocean models and enjoy the theoretical aspects of physical oceanography. I like approaching new problems—not only in science—with an open mind, and I enjoy brainstorming new ideas.

Beyond research, I love spending time in nature—hiking, climbing, and swimming—as well as connecting with people, working on creative projects (including science-inspired ones), reading, and writing.
